Hi! I'm **Deepak G**, an Aerospace Engineering major at IIT Kharagpur. My research focus is on  **safe autonomy**, **reinforcement learning**, and **robotics**.

Last summer, I was a **Summer Undergraduate Research Fellow at Caltech**, where I worked in the AMBER Lab under the supervision of Prof. Aaron Ames, Dr. Yana Lishkova, Dr. Pedro Roque and Dr. Joe Moeller on developing safe and efficient strategies for spacecraft proximity operations. I also worked with the DISCOWER ATMOS free-flyer testbed.

Previously, I worked on reinforcement learning for fixed-wing UAV control at Robotics Research Centre, IIIT Hyderabad and  mathematical modelling of high-altitude airships at CSIR-NAL.
