Hi! I'm **Deepak G**, a Aerospace Engineering Major at IIT Kharagpur. My research interests lie in  **safe autonomy**, **reinforcement learning**, and **robotics**.

I was a **Summer Undergraduate Research Fellow at Caltech** last summer, where I worked in the AMBER Lab under the supervision of Prof. Aaron Ames, Dr. Yana Lishkova, Dr. Pedro Roque and Dr. Joe Moeller on developing safe and efficient stratergies for spacecraft proximity operations. I also worked with the DISCOWER ATMOS free-flyer testbed.

Previously, I worked on reinforcement learning for fixed-wing UAV control at IIIT Hyderabad and  Mathematical Modelling of High-altitude airships at CSIR-NAL.
